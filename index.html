Privacy Policy

Effective as of October 29, 2026

1Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use Slam 2048 (the "Game"), a casual number puzzle game for Android published on Google Play by SONFEDEV ("we", "us", or "our").

We designed the Game so that you can play without telling us who you are. However, the Game may display ads provided by Google AdMob, and that service may process certain information from your device. This policy explains both what we do and what third-party services may do, so you can make an informed choice.

2Information & Data

2.1 Information we collect directly

We do not collect, store, or ask you to provide personal information. The Game does not require account registration or sign-in, and it never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or any other personal details.

We do not operate our own servers to receive or store information about players, and we do not sell, rent, or trade any user information.

2.2 Information processed by third-party services

When ads are shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) built into the Game may automatically collect and process certain information from your device. This processing is carried out by Google, under Google's own policies, and not by us. We do not receive information from Google that identifies you personally. See Section 3 for details.

3Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the Game free, it may display advertisements served by Google AdMob, a mobile advertising service provided by Google LLC and its affiliates ("Google").

To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) or other device identifiers;
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, and network type;
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • Usage and interaction data related to ads, such as ad impressions and clicks;
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash logs or performance information related to the ads SDK.

Google may use this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and — depending on your settings and location — show ads that are personalized to your interests. Depending on your region, you may be shown a consent message asking for your choices about personalized ads before such ads are displayed.

Ads may link to websites or apps operated by third parties. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of advertisers. Please review their policies before providing them with any information.

To learn more about how Google uses data, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses advertising

4Data Storage

We do not have our own servers for storing user information. Game data such as your high scores, progress, and settings, if any, is stored locally on your device only and is not transmitted to us.

  • You can delete this data at any time by clearing the Game's app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the Game.
  • Because we do not hold a copy of your data, we cannot restore lost progress or transfer it to another device.
  • Depending on your device settings, Android's built-in backup feature may include app data in your personal device backup. Such backups are managed by Google and your device, not by us.

5Device Permissions

The Game does not request access to personal data on your device, such as your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or precise location.

To load ads, the Game requires network access (internet permission). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also use the advertising ID permission on supported Android versions so that ads can be delivered and measured. These are standard, non-dangerous permissions that do not give access to your personal content.

6Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party service, which has its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) — https://policies.google.com/privacy

The Game is distributed through Google Play. Your use of Google Play, including downloading and updating the Game, is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy.

We do not control how third parties collect or use data and encourage you to review their policies. If we add other third-party services in the future, we will update this policy accordingly.

7Your Choices

You stay in control of how ads use your device information:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on most Android devices, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID. Menu names may vary by device and Android version.
  • Manage ad personalization: visit Google My Ad Center to control personalized ads from Google.
  • Consent choices: if a consent message was shown to you in the Game, you may be able to change your choices where the Game provides that option.
  • Play offline: the core game may be played without an internet connection; ads are not loaded while your device is offline.
  • Stop all processing: uninstalling the Game stops any further data processing by the Game and the ads SDK on your device.

Because we do not collect or hold personal information, we generally have no personal data to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. For requests regarding data processed by Google, please use the tools provided by Google or contact Google directly.

8Children's Privacy

Slam 2048 is a casual puzzle game intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the Game does not ask any player — of any age — to provide personal information.

As described above, ads in the Game are provided by Google AdMob, which may process device information to serve ads. Parents and guardians should be aware of this and supervise their children's use of mobile apps and devices. You can limit ad personalization and reset the advertising ID using the options in Section 7.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us, and we will take appropriate steps to address your concern.

9Security

Since we do not collect personal information or store user data on our own servers, the risk of your personal information being exposed through us is minimal. Game data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security features. Please keep your device and operating system up to date. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
