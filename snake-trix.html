Privacy Policy

Summary: Snake Trix respects your privacy completely

Snake Trix is designed as an offline game. It does not require account registration, does not collect personal data, does not track your location, and does not transmit any gameplay activity to remote servers.

1 Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy outlines the privacy practices for the mobile game Snake Trix (the "Game") on Android.

Your privacy is paramount. Snake Trix is built from the ground up to be enjoyed completely offline without tracking, surveillance, or unnecessary data harvesting.

2 Zero Personal Data Collected

The Game does NOT collect, gather, record, store, or share any personal information whatsoever. Specifically, Snake Trix does NOT collect:

  • Personal Identifiers: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or account credentials.
  • Government or Financial Data: Credit card numbers, banking info, social security or national IDs.
  • Location Data: Neither precise GPS coordinates nor coarse network-derived location.
  • Sensors & Media: No camera, microphone, photo library, or contact list access.
  • Persistent Identifiers: No Android Advertising IDs (AAID), IMEI, MAC address, or hardware serial numbers are accessed or stored.

3 Local Data Storage

To give you a seamless gaming experience, Snake Trix saves a minimal amount of non-personal game progress data strictly on your local device storage:

  • High scores and personal statistics (e.g., highest score, lines cleared, level reached).
  • Game settings and preferences (e.g., sound effects on/off, background music volume, haptic vibration toggle).
  • Active game state (to let you resume a paused board when reopening the app).

This data resides strictly inside the app's local sandbox (Android SharedPreferences). It is never uploaded to any cloud server or third party. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app data via Android Settings or by uninstalling the application.

4 Device Permissions

Snake Trix does not request or require sensitive Android runtime permissions.

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide subtle haptic feedback during gameplay interactions. This can be toggled on or off inside the in-game settings.

No access to contacts, storage files, location, camera, audio recording, or SMS is ever requested.

5 No Third-Party Trackers

Snake Trix does not embed any third-party behavioral analytics or user tracking SDKs. The game does not transmit your gameplay events to any telemetry endpoint.

6 Children's Privacy

Snake Trix is a casual, family-friendly puzzle game suitable for audiences of all ages, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

Because Snake Trix does not collect, retain, or share any personal information from any user, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play's Families Policy.

7 Google Play Data Safety Compliance

For complete transparency, here is the exact breakdown corresponding to the Google Play Data Safety declarations for Snake Trix:

Question / Category Status Details
Data Collection No data collected The app does not collect any user data.
Data Sharing No data shared No user data is transferred to any third party or external server.
Security Practices Local Isolation Data (scores, settings) remains strictly confined within Android's private app sandbox.

8 Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top of this document will always indicate when the latest revision took effect. Your continued use of Snake Trix constitutes your acceptance of any revisions.
